They all deal with money, but they play completely different games 🏙️

👼 Angels: invest their OWN money, early
🚀 VCs: invest other people's money in high-growth startups
📈 Hedge funds: trade across the financial markets (and profit even when prices fall)
🏛️ Investment banks: don't really invest. They make big deals happen and get paid in fees
🏡 Family offices: manage the wealth of very rich families

The twist? One startup can meet all five on its way from garage to IPO.

Which one would you rather be? 👇
Save this for the next time someone mixes them up 🔖
